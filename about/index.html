T.R. Sithole Attorneys was established on 1 January 2017 and operates from Emalahleni, in the heart of Mpumalanga's coalfields — a town built on hard work and steady industry, values the firm carries into every matter it handles.

As a medium-sized practice, the firm is structured to give each client direct access to experienced legal counsel, rather than being passed through layers of bureaucracy. Every matter, from a maintenance dispute to a commercial contract, is handled with the same standard of care.

The firm's involvement extends beyond the courtroom: T.R. Sithole Attorneys has sponsored local football clubs and tournaments across Mpumalanga, reflecting a continued commitment to the community it serves.
