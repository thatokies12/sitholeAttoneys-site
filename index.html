About the Firm

A practice built on quality, tailored representation.

T.R. Sithole Attorneys was established on 1 January 2017 and operates from Emalahleni, in the heart of Mpumalanga's coalfields — a town built on hard work and steady industry, values the firm carries into every matter it handles.

As a medium-sized practice, the firm is structured to give each client direct access to experienced legal counsel, rather than being passed through layers of bureaucracy. Every matter, from a maintenance dispute to a commercial contract, is handled with the same standard of care.

The firm's involvement extends beyond the courtroom: T.R. Sithole Attorneys has sponsored local football clubs and tournaments across Mpumalanga, reflecting a continued commitment to the community it serves.

Practice Areas

Ten areas of law, one standard of care.

The firm's docket spans civil, criminal, commercial and family law — organised here as it is handled internally, matter by matter.

§ 01

Maintenance Matters

Representation in maintenance court applications, enforcement and variation of existing orders.

§ 02

Divorce Law

Contested and uncontested divorces, settlement agreements, and parenting arrangements.

§ 03

Criminal Law

Defence representation from first appearance through trial, bail applications and appeals.

§ 04

Labour Law

Disciplinary hearings, CCMA referrals, unfair dismissal and workplace disputes.

§ 05

Commercial Contracts

Drafting, review and negotiation of agreements for businesses of every size.

Formation and ongoing administration of family and business trusts.
